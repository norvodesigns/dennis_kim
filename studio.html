03 — Studio

A dedicated educator.

Assistant Professor of Violin at the University of California, Irvine, and on the faculty of the Interlochen Arts Camp each summer.

Teaching runs alongside the season. At UC Irvine's Claire Trevor School of the Arts he holds a full-time appointment; he also teaches at the Orange County School of the Arts and with Junior Chamber Music OC, and serves each summer at Interlochen as Valade Concertmaster of the World Youth Symphony Orchestra.

Former students have gone on to the Curtis Institute of Music, the Colburn School and many other prestigious schools, and now perform with orchestras around the globe.

He regularly serves on competition panels internationally, most recently as an adjudicator for the Dallas International Violin Competition.

Philosophy

He teaches the same standard he was taught: consistent, daily practice, approached as if the goal were a professional career — whether or not it becomes one.
